Contrast

Contrast helps visitors distinguish important content from the rest of the page. It can be created with color, size, shape, or spacing.

Callout

Use strong contrast between foreground and background colors so that everyone can read the message.

  • Alignment: Organize related content along a common edge.
  • Repetition: Repeat visual styles to create consistency.
  • Proximity: Group related items together.
  • White space: Leave room around content to improve focus.
Good design makes information easier to understand.
